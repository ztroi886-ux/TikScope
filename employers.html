How we work

Four steps from brief to arrival

Every campaign follows the same route, so you always know what stage your vacancies are at.

  1. 01

    Brief and shortlist

    You describe the roles, salary and start date. We return a shortlist from our candidate pool, usually within a week.

  2. 02

    Screening and interviews

    Skill tests, reference checks and language screening. You interview the final candidates in person or online.

  3. 03

    Documents and permits

    Contracts, medical certificates, translations, work permits and visa appointments prepared and tracked by our office.

  4. 04

    Travel and onboarding

    Flights, arrival and first-week orientation coordinated with your HR team so the start date holds.
