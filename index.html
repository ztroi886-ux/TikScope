    About us

    Built by people who made the same journey

    Aryos Group is an international recruitment company working with candidates from the Middle East. We match people with verified employers across Europe and North America, and we stay with the file until they have arrived and started work.

    Going abroad for work is one of the biggest decisions a family makes, and it is usually made with incomplete information. Too many people pay money to an agent and never hear back. We built Aryos Group around the opposite promise: a real contract, a stated salary, a named case officer, and a file you can check yourself at any time.

    Every employer, salary and contract on this website is checked before the role is published. Documents, translations, medical checks, appointments and work permits are handled by our office in Erbil, and every message is answered by a person within 48 hours.
