Understanding your status

What each status means

Under Review
Your file is complete and with the consulate or the employer. Nothing is needed from you — we will contact you when the decision arrives.
Need More Documents
Something is missing or unclear. The missing items are listed in your result box. Send them by WhatsApp or bring the originals to the office.
Approved
The permit or employer approval is granted. Your case officer will book the visa appointment and confirm your travel window.
Issued
The visa is in your passport or sent to your email. Keep a printed copy with you when you travel.
